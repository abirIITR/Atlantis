Worked active sonar for 11 years, so take this for what it's worth. The formation in that crop isn't a bait ball, and it's too tight and too fast for a thermocline artifact. Could still be equipment ghosting depending on the rig, hard to say without the raw file.

What I will say is the spacing between the returns is oddly even. Biological contacts don't usually hold spacing like that unless something is herding them, or they're moving with intent as a group. Not saying I know what it is. Just saying it's a weird one.
